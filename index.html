⚠️ Important Compatibility Notes

✅ For Best Experience:

  • Use Desktop/Laptop with Chrome browser
  • Stable Internet Connection recommended
  • Microphone Access required for voice recognition

⚠️ Mobile Limitations:

  • iPhone/iPad: Only Auto-scroll mode works (no voice recognition)
  • Android: Voice recognition may work but performance varies
  • Mobile browsers: Limited Web Speech API support

🌐 Browser Compatibility:

  • Chrome/Chromium: ✅ Recommended - best performance
  • Edge: ✅ Good performance
  • Safari: ⚠️ Limited functionality, no voice recognition on iOS
  • Firefox: ❌ No Web Speech API support

💡 Tips for Best Results

🎤 Voice Recognition:

  • Use Chrome browser on Desktop/Laptop for optimal performance
  • External microphones provide better accuracy than built-in mics
  • Minimize background noise for improved tracking
  • Speak at natural pace with clear pronunciation
  • Recording with a co-host? Start their lines with >> or @Name: — they show dimmed and voice tracking skips straight to your next line

⚙️ Technical:

  • Stable internet connection required for voice recognition (auto-scroll works offline)
  • HTTPS connection required for microphone access in production
  • Allow microphone access when prompted
  • For mobile: Use Auto-scroll mode (P key)
  • Install as an app: click the install icon in Chrome's address bar for a standalone window

💾 Data Storage & Privacy

Smart Teleprompter automatically saves your data locally in your browser:

  • Settings: Font size, colors, speed, margins, and language preferences
  • Script Content: Your teleprompter text is preserved between sessions
  • Local Storage: All data stays on your device (scripts are only uploaded if you explicitly use the Share feature, and shared copies auto-delete after 30 days)
  • Privacy: Your scripts and settings stay on your device. No Google Analytics, no cookies — only cookieless, anonymous page-view counts via Cloudflare Web Analytics

✅ Your data is private and secure - stored only on your device!
